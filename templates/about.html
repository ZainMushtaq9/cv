About ResumeAI

ResumeAI is an AI-powered Resume–Job Description Union Analyzer that helps job seekers optimize their resumes for specific positions.

Our system performs a set-union analysis between your resume keywords and job description requirements, giving you three critical insights:

  • ✅ Matched Keywords: Skills you already have that the job wants.
  • ❌ Missing Keywords: Gaps you need to fill in your resume.
  • ➕ Extra Keywords: Skills in your resume not in the JD — keep or remove.

We use advanced AI models to understand context, semantic equivalences, and Pakistan's education system for accurate scoring.

Built with ❤️ using Flask, Groq AI, and modern web technologies.
